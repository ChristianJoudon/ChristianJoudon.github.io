A service business does not sell a box. It sells the right person, at the right time, with confidence.
The product promise

For customers, booking should feel effortless. For the business, effortless requires orchestration: real availability, the right team member, clear terms, protected time, dependable communication, and money that lands where it belongs.

Chime makes that complexity disappear. The customer sees a concise, branded path to a confirmed appointment. The business sees one reliable operating picture behind it.

01 / CONVERT

Make it easy to say yes.

Publish real openings in a guided experience that works beautifully on the device already in the customer’s hand.

02 / PROTECT

Treat time like inventory.

Match services to qualified team members, prevent conflicting reservations, and secure commitment with configurable deposits.

03 / OPERATE

Run the day from one view.

Bring schedules, requests, customer context, payments, and communications together without stitching through separate tools.

Payments & trust

Protect the appointment. Keep the money clear.

Deposits should create commitment, not accounting confusion. Chime makes each state visible and routes funds through the business’s own connected Stripe account.

  • Business-owned from the startConnected payments go directly to the business rather than being pooled inside Chime.
  • Authorize, collect, release, or refundEvery action follows the appointment and remains visible in one ledger.
  • Confirmed by the payment systemWebhook reconciliation keeps Chime aligned with what Stripe actually completed.
  • Safe against accidental repeatsIdempotent operations protect customers and businesses from duplicate charges.
One Chime, many businesses

A shared platform with private boundaries.

Every business receives its own identity, rules, team, customers, payment connection, and trusted domains. Chime shares the power of one product without blurring who anything belongs to.

01 / IDENTITY

Unmistakably theirs

Each business has its own name, visual system, booking address, language, currency, time zone, and embed permissions.

02 / OPERATIONS

Rules that fit the work

Services, durations, team qualifications, availability, notice windows, policies, and deposits are configured per business.

03 / OWNERSHIP

Data stays in its lane

Business identity travels with every request, and database relationships enforce separation rather than relying on convention.

04 / SCALE

One product, consistent quality

A central platform delivers upgrades, reliability, and operational tooling while every customer-facing experience remains distinct.
