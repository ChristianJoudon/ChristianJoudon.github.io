About

Software and technical writing.

I work across product design, databases, APIs, and user interfaces. My projects range from full-stack applications to tools that reduce repetitive work.

At Hawaii Life, I bring hands-on reservation and accounting experience into that work. I build tools for email intake, record entry, document collection, and review, then turn the operating process into walkthroughs and quick references.

My technical writing starts with how a system behaves: what information it needs, which decisions it supports, and what someone needs to know to use it. The same attention shapes my product explanations, research essays, and interactive data stories.

Creative writing, photography, and visual design are also part of my practice. They inform how I structure an explanation, pace an interface, and make a complex idea easier to follow.

Operational Workflows

The work around the software.

At Hawaii Life, accounting work reveals where information gets separated: an email, a spreadsheet, a document, and an entry in another system. I build tools and write guidance that reconnect those steps, with clear points for staff review.

Email → reservation → saved record

Reservation entry and updates

AI-assisted workflows turn booking emails into prepared reservation details, then use JavaScript browser automation to enter and update records in Escapia. Channel-specific parsers handle Airbnb, Booking.com, Marriott/HVMI, and direct bookings.

Exact property matching, financial reconciliation, and saved-state checks keep each action tied to its source. Payments and other final financial submissions stay with a person; parser QA covers 66 spreadsheet cases.

JavaScript · Browser automation · Google Sheets · Data validation

Email → review → accounting entry

Invoice preparation

An email-to-accounting dashboard retrieves messages, identifies invoices, and brings extracted details, draft accounting memos, and source files into one table.

Staff can compare the prepared information with the original invoice before copying it into Escapia. The workflow supports that review and entry step; direct integration is planned around future platform updates.

Email processing · AI extraction · Review interface · Accounting memos

Property record → request → document

Insurance renewal and collection

An Apps Script workbench connects property records, renewal outreach, certificate collection, and a responsive upload portal. Unit validation, upload checks, and duplicate detection help keep incoming documents attached to the right record.

Drive storage and Sheets synchronization carry documents back into the workflow. Personalized owner correspondence explains what to provide and where to upload it, with staff review built into the process.

Apps Script · Google Sheets · Upload portal · Owner correspondence

Guest record → receipt → email draft

Refund correspondence

Refund report ingestion prepares guest-specific Gmail drafts and matches supporting receipts in Drive. Recipient mapping and batch labels keep the prepared correspondence together for accounting review.

Compressed, chunked storage with SHA-256 integrity checks supports larger report batches while preserving existing reports and completed correspondence.

Read the workflow ↗
Payment report → receipts → review packet

ACH document processing

A recurring Python pipeline assembles multipage receipts, uses OCR to identify supporting information, matches vendors, and reconciles payment totals. Validated packets and manifests feed an Apps Script queue for Gmail drafts.

The document pipeline combines qpdf, Ghostscript, and Tesseract, with 34 automated processor tests.

Python · OCR · qpdf · Tesseract · Apps Script

Source document → consistent record

Document and memo standards

Invoice processing also needs consistent language. I develop AI-assisted workflows for normalizing document names and accounting descriptions, supported by practical staff guides.

The instructions explain how to prepare the information, what to check against the source, and where a judgment still needs a person. Short references make those standards easier to return to during the work.

Technical writing in practice

Guidance beside the task.

I authored nine illustrated operational handbooks totaling more than 300 pages for Hawaii Life’s reservations, accounting, refunds, and insurance work. Annotated screenshots, linked references, decision paths, and exception handling help staff follow a process and find their way back after an interruption.

The format depends on the moment: a fuller walkthrough for learning a process, or a brief reference for finding the next step.
